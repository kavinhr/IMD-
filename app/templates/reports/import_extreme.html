Format Requirements

The parser expects the standard export format:

  • A heading identifying the station (e.g. 1. CHENNAI (N)).
  • A subheading identifying the month (e.g. EXTREME WEATHER EVENTS IN THE MONTH OF APRIL).
  • A table containing historical data, with the first two rows acting as headers.
  • Values and Dates formatted as Value (Date) (e.g. 39.2 (13)).
  • An ALL TIME RECORD row containing deep historical records, formatted as Value (Date, Year) (e.g. 42.8 (27, 1908)).
This tool extracts data into a dedicated shadow table. It will not alter your master observation database.
